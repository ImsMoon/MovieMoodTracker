# 📖 User Guide

## Welcome to MoodFlix!

This guide will walk you through everything you need to know to get started with MoodFlix — from signing up to understanding your mood patterns.

---

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Step 1: Sign Up

When you first open MoodFlix, you'll see the login screen.

**Options:**
- **Google** — Sign in with your Google account
- **Apple** — Sign in with your Apple ID
- **Facebook** — Sign in with Facebook
- **Demo Mode** — Skip login and try as a demo user

**To sign up:**
1. Click your preferred login method
2. Authorize the app (if using social login)
3. You'll be taken to the Discover screen

> 💡 **Tip**: You can also connect IMDB and Rotten Tomatoes from the login screen to sync your ratings later.

---

## 🎴 Discovering Movies

### The Swipe Interface

The main screen shows a **Tinder-style card stack** of movies and series.

**How to use:**
1. **Swipe Right** (or tap green ✓ button) → "I've seen this" → Opens rating flow
2. **Swipe Left** (or tap pink ♡ button) → Add to wishlist
3. **Tap Info button** (blue ℹ) → View movie details
4. **Tap More Info** on the card → Full details modal

### Categories

At the top, you can switch between:
- **Trending** — What's popular this week
- **Movies** — Popular movies
- **Series** — Popular TV shows
- **Top Rated** — Highest rated content

### Infinite Scroll

Keep swiping! The app automatically loads more content as you go. There's no limit — swipe as much as you want.

---

## 🎭 Rating a Movie

When you swipe right or tap "Seen it", a **3-step rating flow** opens.

### Step 1: Select Your Mood

You'll see 10 mood options:

| | |
|---|---|
| 😊 Happy | 😢 Sad |
| 😌 Relaxed | 😰 Anxious |
| ⚡ Energetic | 😑 Bored |
| 🥰 Romantic | 🌅 Nostalgic |
| 🗺️ Adventurous | 🤔 Thoughtful |

**Tap the mood** that best describes how you feel right now or how you felt while watching.

### Step 2: Rate the Movie

- **Drag stars** or tap to rate from 1-10
- **Optional**: Write a short review
- Tap **Continue** to proceed

### Step 3: Add Details (Optional)

This step is entirely optional but helps build better insights:

**Watched Date:**
- Select **Year** (required to enable month)
- Select **Month** (required to enable day)
- Select **Day**

**Time of Day:**
- 🌅 Morning
- ☀️ Afternoon
- 🌆 Evening
- 🌙 Night

Tap **Save & Sync ✨** to save your rating.

> 💡 **Tip**: The more details you provide, the better your mood insights will be!

## 📅 Viewing Your Mood Map

Navigate to **Mood Map** from the navbar.

### The Heatmap

You'll see a **GitHub-style calendar** showing your entire year:
- Each colored square = a day you watched something
- Color represents your mood that day
- Hover over a square to see details

### Filters

**By Year:**
- Click year buttons at the top to switch years
- See how your moods change year over year

**By Mood:**
- Click mood filter buttons to highlight specific moods
- See where you felt happy, sad, relaxed, etc.

### Monthly Breakdown

Below the heatmap, you'll see:
- **12 months** with dominant mood emoji
- **Watch count** per month
- **Top genre** per month

## 🔄 Syncing with IMDB & Rotten Tomatoes

### Connecting IMDB
1. Go to Profile
2. Tap "Connect" next to IMDB
3. Choose login method (Amazon/Google/Facebook)
4. Authorize the connection

### Connecting Rotten Tomatoes
1. Go to Profile
2. Tap "Connect" next to Rotten Tomatoes
3. Choose login method (Google/Facebook/Apple)
4. Authorize the connection

### Syncing Ratings
**Per-movie sync:**
1. Go to Rated view
2. Find the movie
3. Tap "IMDB" or "RT" button
4. Wait for sync confirmation

**Bulk sync:**
1. Go to Rated view
2. Tap "Sync All" button
3. All unsynced ratings will be pushed

## ❓ FAQ

### Is my data private?
Yes! All data is stored locally on your device. We don't send your personal viewing data to any servers.

### Can I use MoodFlix without signing up?
Yes! Tap "Skip → Continue as Demo User" to try without an account.

### How accurate are the vendor insights?
Insights are based on your personal data. The more you rate, the more accurate they become.

### Can I export my data?
Data export is coming in a future update. Currently, all data is stored in your browser's LocalStorage.

### What happens if I clear my browser data?
All your ratings, wishlist, and mood data will be lost. Make sure to sync important ratings to IMDB/RT first.

### Can I use MoodFlix on mobile?
Yes! The app is fully responsive and works on phones, tablets, and desktops.

### How do I report a bug?
Email us at hello@moodflix.app or open an issue on GitHub.
